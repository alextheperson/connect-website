Turn 0 - Empty Board

TERMINOLOGY
There are three layers (in this example, we are playing 3-deep 3x3 fractal tic-tac-toe with two players)
  /\    The meta-board - this is the WHOLE board. Three in a row on this wins the game
 /  \   The super-boards - there are 9 of these (3x3). Three-in-a-row on one of these puts a token on the meta-board
/    \  The sub-boards - There are 81 of these (9x9). Three-in-a-row on one of these puts a token on a super-board, which moves play to the corrosponing super-board.
